About Me

I am a PhD researcher in the Department of Electrical and Electronic Engineering at Imperial College London, where I am fortunate to be supervised by Professor Cong Ling. My research focuses on quantum algorithms for sampling problems, with particular interest in applications to lattice cryptanalysis. This website contains my publications, GitHub repositories, talks, and CV.

Research Interests

  • Quantum algorithms
  • Lattice cryptanalysis
  • Lattice Gaussian sampling
  • Markov chain Monte Carlo methods
